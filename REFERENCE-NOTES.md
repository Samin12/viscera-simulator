## Visibility and case design

The simulator should make tissue coverage meaningful. Showing the whole biliary tree as exposed, brightly coloured tubes would teach a misleading visual expectation. Use optional educational overlays for concealed anatomy. Omentum, fat and adhesions should obstruct the view until they have been moved or addressed in the simulation. This is a design inference from the photographs, not a patient-specific reconstruction.

The [SAGES multi-society Safe Cholecystectomy guideline](https://www.sages.org/publications/guidelines/safe-cholecystectomy-multi-society-practice-guideline/) supports anatomic identification using the critical view of safety and a pause before clipping or dividing ductal/arterial structures. Its recommendations vary in evidence strength; many are conditional or expert opinion. The simulation can represent recognition of uncertainty, requests for help, and stopping a difficult case as meaningful decisions. Do not make “remove the gallbladder at any cost” the only successful outcome.

Faculty must validate anatomy, variation, target recognition, feedback and scoring before formal trainee assessment.

## Verified representative instrument set

The [official Intuitive X/Xi instrument and accessory catalogue](https://www.intuitive.com/en-us/-/media/ISI/Intuitive/Pdf/da-vinci-x-xi-instruments-accessories-catalog.pdf), pages 3–6, lists:

| Family | Examples suitable for representing selectable tool types |
| --- | --- |
| Graspers | ProGrasp forceps; Cadiere forceps; Tip-Up fenestrated grasper |
| Bipolar instruments | Maryland bipolar forceps; Fenestrated bipolar forceps; Curved bipolar dissector |
| Monopolar instruments | Monopolar curved scissors (Hot Shears); Permanent cautery hook; Permanent cautery spatula |
| Clip appliers | Medium-large and large polymer clip appliers; small titanium clip applier |
| Scissors | Round tip scissors; Potts scissors |
| Suturing | Large needle driver; SutureCut needle drivers |
| Fluid management | EndoWrist suction irrigator |
| Advanced energy | Vessel Sealer Extend; SynchroSeal; Harmonic ACE curved shears |

These are representative named products, not interchangeable capabilities or universal choices for every structure. Availability and compatibility depend on platform, market, generator, accessories and software. The source separately specifies clip compatibility. Avoid unverified numerical generator settings or claims that any energy instrument can safely divide any duct.

The [current Intuitive instrument page](https://www.intuitive.com/en-us/products-and-services/da-vinci/instruments) specifically identifies its wristed suction irrigator as surgeon-console controlled. Therefore, suction need not be depicted only as an assistant tool. Describe the requested “Bovie” function in the UI as **monopolar energy**, associated with an appropriate selected instrument.

A specimen bag is a retrieval accessory, not an EndoWrist jaw type. A [published robotic cholecystectomy study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4620793/) describes separate assistant access and a specimen bag for removal. This prototype may represent bag deployment/retrieval as an assistant action, while making clear that actual port and team workflows vary.

## Product boundary

Photographic reference can improve colour, occlusion, texture and layout. It does not provide deformable tissue mechanics, validated force or energy response, stereo depth, da Vinci controller/pedal behaviour, or clinical competence assessment. The browser version represents selected interactions. It must not claim to reproduce all da Vinci tools, certify readiness for surgery, or provide live operative guidance.
